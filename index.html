ZENITH RULES

You woke in the Underworld. The surface is 55 meters of hostile strata above you. Every layer is fused: Corruption and Crimson bleed into each other, Not the Bees hives leak honey into lava, and The Constant drains the light from your torch.

Mine Hellstone, Demonite and Crimtane. Smash Life Crystals to grow stronger. Build workstations to unlock the deeper recipes. Summon the three trials at the Ritual Altar beside your spawn.

HAZARDS

Lava Slimes leave molten pools on death. Demon Eyes hunt in packs. Lava inflicts burning damage. Falling more than 7 meters hurts.
